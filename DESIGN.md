# Zaman Optics design: Shade Picker

Modern sunglasses store. Shoppers start from lens colour or frame shape.

- Accent #0F766E (hover #0B5F59), ink #1D1D1F, surface #F5F5F7, white canvas.
- Radius 10px, restrained shadows, spacious sections (72px desktop, 56px mobile).
- Fonts: Archivo 700 for headings, Manrope 400 for body (settings_data.json).
- Homepage: shade-hero, shade-promise, shade-picker (lens swatches + shape tiles), product rows, shade-categories, clip-ons, eyeglasses, kids, contact lenses, FAQ.
- All copy and collections are section settings/blocks; styles live in assets/zaman-shade.css (sh-* classes), loaded after the older zaman CSS.
- Filter URLs: /collections/sunglasses/lens-<colour> and /shape-<shape> (tag-based).
- Do not add brand names to public copy.
